# Brand — نبرة · Nabra

**Chosen name: نبرة / Nabra** — "tone of voice". The product turns a *voice* into motion; the
name says exactly that in Arabic, and is easy to say in French and English (NAH-bra). Short
(5 letters), unique in the reel-tool space, nothing in common with "Make it".

To rename: edit `src/config/brand.ts` (name, tagline, colours). Title, manifest, favicon and
the logo wordmark all read from there.

## The 10 candidates
| # | Name | Why / why not |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nabra · نبرة** | ✅ chosen — voice/tone, 2 syllables, works in AR/FR/EN |
| 2 | Rawi · راوي | "narrator" — strong, but many apps use it |
| 3 | Sada · صدى | "echo" — nice, but hard to own as a .com and soft in Latin |
| 4 | Kadra · كادرة | from Darija/French "cadre" (frame) — fun, but sounds like "cadre" (manager) in FR |
| 5 | Moja · موجة | "wave" — cute, but means "fashion" in Spanish slang |
| 6 | Lamha · لمحة | "glimpse" — elegant, hard "h" for FR/EN speakers |
| 7 | Qissa · قصة | "story" — the "Q" is hard for non-Arabic speakers |
| 8 | Harka · حركة | "motion" — great meaning but "harka" carries a heavy historical meaning in Algeria |
| 9 | Zaha · زها | "bloom/brightness" — strongly associated with Zaha Hadid |
| 10 | Sawti · صوتي | "my voice" — too literal, weak as an international mark |

## Logo
The mark is the letter **ن** (noon, first letter of نبرة): its bowl is drawn as audio waveform
bars and its dot is a "record" pulse — voice becoming shape. Geometry lives in
`src/config/logo.ts` (shared by the React logo, favicon and manifest icon).
